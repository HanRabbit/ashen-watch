# Ashen Watch · Rodin production register

## Tool capability and budget

| Capability | Supported workflow and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Gen-2.5 | Text, one to five uploaded reference images, or both. Exports GLB, USDZ, FBX, OBJ or STL. The requested polygon count is a target, not an acceptance result: Raw accepts 500–1,000,000; Quad accepts 1,000–50,000. Optional texture de-lighting aims to reduce baked highlights. |
| Agentic (beta) | Requires one to five uploaded reference images; a text prompt is optional. Prefer it when the defining geometry can be built from editable n-gons, profiles or primitives. It selects either the Agentic n-gon or the Rodin high-poly pipeline and exports GLB, USDZ, FBX or STL. Neither reference fidelity nor editable structure is guaranteed. |
| Optional paid operations | BANG for part separation, PolyGen for a low-poly derivative with optional textures, and rigging/skinning with optional animation. These create separate results; none was required for the flask. |
| Retrieval and acceptance | Create a signed image upload and PUT the exact bytes; submit once, then check status and retrieve the result. Check the result rather than resubmitting on an unknown status; do not automatically retry a timed-out submission. Record the permanent result page, not signed transfer URLs. Inspect the actual binary, geometry, UVs and maps before integration. A hardware-rendered after pass is a separate visual acceptance gate. Separated parts, translucency and exact packing are inspection criteria, not generation guarantees. |

## AW–B–01 — Corded lamp-oil flask (B, interactive secondary)

**Approved reference:** `concept/04-props-and-shade.png` cropped at (441,637), 294×360 native pixels. Only the flask is supplied. **Art direction:** damp, austere gatehouse, working equipment rather than ornament. One 0.26 m tall vessel, squat irregular pear body about 0.18 m wide, stable foot, short neck and slightly eccentric stopper. Warm muted olive/amber glass around old oil, dark matte twisted hemp knotted into four or five uneven diamonds, a collar and abraded porous cork. No trail of rope, logo, carrying handle, fire, plinth, bright baked glints or painted ground shadow. Silhouette, real cord crossings and nodes matter above microscopic weave. Expect correctly unpackable UVs/normals and roughness, nonmetallic glass/cork/hemp, restrained reflectance; no bloom or emissive paint. Production goal ≤6,000 triangles, one shared local GLB, ≤1K packed maps and shared instances. Preserve the current bottom origin and pickup logic. Keep the old mesh if loading or quality checks fail. Category B budget: **one reference-guided generation**; no paid refinement unless there is a diagnosed defect.

**Material:** The source base colour was largely grey; the knotwork and cork were already present. The export uses the source roughness and UV-baked height to separate the smoked upper glass and muted amber oil below a soft fill line from the matte hemp and porous cork. The lossless data atlas uses **R** for glass clearcoat weight (`KHR_materials_clearcoat`), **G** for roughness and **B** = 0 for metalness. It is an opaque, nonmetallic approximation of an oil-filled vessel: no transmission, whole-object alpha blending or emission. Rebuild with `tools/pack-oil-flask.py` (NumPy and Pillow).

**Fit and integration:** Source is `+Y` up; the main knot faces local `+Z`. The export bakes in metres, centres X/Z, and puts the foot at `Y=0`. Fitted X/Y/Z extents are **0.192 × 0.260 × 0.19195 m**. World-facing directions in Three.js: bailey shelf `−Z` (retains its `0.8×` scale), supply crate `+Z`, B1 table east `+X`. One load serves three instances sharing geometry, material and textures; no additional LOD. The existing parent transforms, visibility and pickup conditions are unchanged. A failed load or runtime asset check retains the procedural fallback.

**Quality status:** Static and integration checks passed. The original GLB decoded with finite positions, unit normals, consistent face winding, populated UV0 with no collapsed UV faces, and three readable PBR maps. The export was independently re-imported; byte-for-byte repacking, fitted bounds, map colour-space bindings, nonmetallic/matte mask checks, shared loading and fallback all passed. The four dedicated tests, full suite (248 tests), typecheck and production build passed at integration. **GPU follow-camera check: passed.** The before/after B1 walks inspected three sides of the flask and a close crop. The E interaction removed the flask and increased inventory from 0 to 1; the table edge stayed in place. The largest before/after camera translation was 4.6 cm. Reproduce with `tools/oil-flask-walk.mjs`.

## AW–A–02 — Gatehouse roof and outer timber gable (A, scene architecture, production priority)

The entrance building is seen on every return and from the wall walk. The current main roof is two tiled boxes and two plain triangular gables. Replace **only the exterior roof assembly**. Preserve the authored gatehouse, hearth, door, window liner, bell bracket, smoke socket and interior rafters. Use the existing two-slope silhouette and a single modest front cross-gable informed by the approved gatehouse in `concept/01-castle.png`; the traced native-resolution reference isolates its slate and exposed timber. Physical envelope ~7.9 m wide × 10 m long; eaves near 3.55 m and ridge about 5.7 m. Cushion between compressed roof and character headroom; no new collider. Slate courses should lap, vary in chipped edges, give narrow shadows, avoid even fish scales or a uniform emboss. Rain-washed charcoal slate has a low, broad sheen. Timber: irregular oak end-grain, silvery brown boards in the front gable, dark joints. Restrained lichen in a damp sheltered edge, no fantasy ornament. No towers, windows, gate, walls, people, ground or chimney furnished by the generator. Single visual GLB, target 18k tris, web-sized base/normal/roughness with no baked sky highlights. Fit and inspect the underside, both ends and top locally; retain gameplay geometry on load failure. One generation for this assembly. Do not generate the watchman or ghosts.

**Roof production record.** The approved reference crop is `gatehouse-roof-reference.jpg`. One Rodin Agentic generation (`1fa2177a-030e-483e-9684-d62e47222998`) produced 41,224 triangles in 282 primitives. Local fitting removed the duplicate interior structure, joined the slate courses and installed a light-tight timber liner. The final `public/models/rodin/gatehouse-roof.glb` is 1,261,992 bytes, 19,666 triangles, three indexed draws and two opaque PBR materials. It measures 7.945 × 2.361 × 9.987 m in scene axes. Embedded images comprise 128² slate and 256² oak colour, normal and roughness maps. The courtyard truss faces positive simulation Z. The renderer validates the file before replacing the fallback. Day gameplay coverage was recorded from the back of the room, the eave and the rampart.
